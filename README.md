# The Ripple Effect — Illustrated edition

An interactive MGT402 flipbook about how generative AI is changing creative industries, intellectual property, and the people and businesses connected to them.

**Public link:** https://karthikeyagit28.github.io/Flipbook/

## What the flipbook covers

- How generative AI is trained on creative work and the developing copyright approaches in the US, Europe, the UK, Germany, and India.
- Questions of authorship, ownership, licensing, creative work, and the economics of AI-generated content.
- Case studies on Getty Images v. Stability AI and the WGA and SAG-AFTRA labor agreements.
- Practical steps for businesses, including licensed data, human oversight, updated contracts, and market-specific policies.
- Broader effects on creative livelihoods, education, data-center resources, energy use, and the environment.
- A sources and references section for further reading.

## Using the flipbook

- Choose a section from the contents menu.
- Turn pages with the on-screen controls, the lower page corners, or the left and right arrow keys.
- Use **Full screen** for an expanded view.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. The flipbook is self-contained and does not require a build step.
